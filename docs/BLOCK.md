## What the page does (built 2026-11-10)

- The turn flag `block: true` (scenario **"Warrior wins, the monster blocks two strikes"**, `MBLOCK`: a crit that is blocked, then a plain hit that is blocked, then the finish). A blocked hit: `blockAmount` = amount x (1 - BLOCK_REDUCE), never a crit (`CRIT` is dropped, the log says "the critical hit is cancelled" and there is no crit shake), the number reads **BLOCK 16** (class `.num.block`), the log says "(blocked: 20% less damage)".
- `blockFx(T, sd, tHit)`: a short-lived layer drawn by the Barrier's own renderer `brDraw`. `brState` / `brDraw` got an optional `L.geo = { sd, r, cx, gy, vis }`: the blocker's size and place instead of the mage's (the circle's bottom ON `gy`, the glass and the ripple clipped at the ground line, the ground ring flattened to 5% so it hardly leaves the floor line, a fixed visibility 0.85). The layer: it snaps up in `BLOCK_CAST_MS` 110 ms (the cast's climbing arc and flash), holds `BLOCK_HOLD_MS` 380 ms, lets go over `BLOCK_DROP_MS` 380 ms; the ripple (the flash, the bright arc on the rim and the spreading ring) is at the blow. Its radius is a share of the blocker's cell: a monster uses `MONSTERS[key].block.r` (the Elite 0.36: it is low and wide), a character 0.5 of a character width (like the mage's own).
- `blockSpec()` / `blockPre()` / `monBlock()`: a monster's block comes from `MONSTERS[key].block = { pack, order, holds, fps, idx }`. The pack may be a strip of its own (`slot block`, not built yet) or **frames of another pack played in an order**: **the Elite's Block = the Stone Guard's frames 2, 3, 4, 5, 3, 6 at 14 fps** (the shield floats up in front of the face; the face frame is the block frame, the 3rd played; it lands about 165 ms after the start). While it plays `MON.blocking` stops `monOnHp` from playing the hit pack.
- A **Block (loops)** preview button in the monster preview row (shown for a monster that has `block`).
- **Wired (the blocker is a monster with `block`, the Elite so far):** the warrior's attack (`act()`: also with the Thunderclap lightning skill: the bolt still shows), the Rogue's Shadow Shiv, Static Stiletto, Winter's Kiss and Permafrost Execution (`rogueAct` hands `block` to `act()`), and the Mage's casts (`castAct`: the blocker's pack starts early so its guard is up when the spell arrives; the spell's own impact still plays on the glass). **The two backstabs too (the user, 2026-11-10: 'Yes, blockable'):** the Umbral Shroud (`backstabAct`) and the Abyssal Backstab (`abyssalAct`) start the blocker's pack early (so the guard is up at the strike), cut the damage, show BLOCK n and put the glass on the side the blow comes from, which is BEHIND the target (`-T.dir`: the far side); the Elite's guard pose is still in front (the glass makes it read). **Not wired:** the monsters' attacks on a character, and a character BLOCKING (no character has a block pack yet: a blocked monster blow on the Warrior would only show the glass, the number and the log). Wiring a new attack path is a few lines: `blockSpec` + `monBlock` early, `blockAmount`, no crit, `blockFx`.
