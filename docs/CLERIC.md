## What the game says the Cleric is

A hybrid (physical and magical attack) **defensive tank** with the highest HP and END in the game: holy and dark skills, reflect barriers, +10 Block Rate and +10 Accuracy as class bonuses. It has **no pure heal skill**: it heals through Radiant Smite (40% of the damage it deals) and Unholy Siphon (steals HP). The weakest class in the damage race, the strongest in party content (the Templar).

Two branches, picked at a level and permanent: **Templar** (mace, holy, takes damage meant for allies) and **Occultist** (wand, dark, curses, pays its own HP). Both can carry a **shield or an orb** in the off hand.

**The rule from the Rogue's branches (`class-branches-design`):** every base-class skill must be performable by BOTH branches.
