## Rules for every Cleric skill

1. **One weapon, two branches.** The weapon is in the FAR hand (the main hand, behind the body): the MACE (Templar) or the WAND (Occultist); the NEAR hand (the off hand, in front) carries the SHIELD or the ORB and stays in its guard at the chest unless a skill needs it. Every base skill must be performable by BOTH branches (a branch keeps the base class's skills, `docs/RESKIN_PILOT_PLAN.md`), so the gestures must work with a club and with a wand: a raised arm, a downward stroke, a thrust, a pointing hand; nothing that only a club can do.
2. **The sheet is bare fists in poses.** No weapon, shield, light or glow in the art; the items are layers drawn on the user's hand marks (the near-hand rule and the mace/wand pictures: `docs/CLERIC.md`), the effects are code.
3. **Every panel says what BOTH arms do.** The generators mix the arms up from panel to panel (the Warrior's one-handed Sunder Strike prompt, `docs/WARRIOR_SKILLS.md` round 8), and the Cleric's two hands do different things by design, so each frame names the near fist's place and the far fist's height (as a share of his height H).
4. **No buffs and no debuffs** (the user's rule of 2026-11-13): Purified, Reduce Magic Def, Weaken, Condemned, Bonded, Warded and the rest are not designed or drawn for now. The barrier skills (3 and 6) are the exception in spirit only: their barrier is the Mage's Barrier glass look and a visible effect, not a status icon.
5. **Holy and dark effects are code,** reusing what exists: the holy palette and effects of the Slime King (`HOLY_PAL`, `hlBoltDraw`, `hlImpact`, `healAct`, the golden light of Royal Restoration), `holy_aura`, `dark_aura` and the dark slime's effects, the Mage's Barrier (`brDraw`), the heal number (`.num.heal`), the holy damage number (`.num.holy`) and the HUD's heal flash.
6b. **The swinging arm must be allowed to be HIDDEN (learned 2026-11-21, Radiant Smite v1).** The prompt said near and far in every panel and the sheet still came back with the arms swapped in four panels of six: a far arm swung back or raised hides behind the torso, the head and the near arm, and the generator does not want to hide the action, so it moved the near arm out of the way and showed that one swinging. The fix (v2): describe the two arms by what you SEE (the near arm = the big pauldron, the ring-mail sleeve and the forearm across his chest, in front; the far arm = comes out from behind his torso, partly hidden), say that the hiding is correct and required, say which part of the far arm shows in each swinging panel, and tell it that the panel is wrong if the arm lying across his chest is the one that swings. Check every sheet for it BEFORE cutting: look at which arm has the pauldron in front. (A variant, Sanctified Judgment panel 2: the raised arm was drawn out of the NEAR shoulder while a second arm lay across the chest, so the man had two near arms: count the arms in every panel, exactly ONE across the chest and the acting one coming out from behind.)
6. **Sheets** are ChatGPT 3 x 2, ONE small reference picture of the man (his standing pose is not to be copied), plain closed fists, no thumb paragraph (the Warrior's later prompts); the figure asked at about 50% of the panel (ChatGPT draws him about 1.4 times bigger), the head 7.5%, a 14% margin, grounded skills with the floor at 80%.
