# The in-stage HUD (2026-11-12)

The user (2026-11-12), with a reference picture of a versus screen from another game (a round portrait at each end, the HP bars running in to a big **VS** in the middle, a name above each bar, a caption under it):
*'Can you design HP bars to be shown in the stage like that? Player/enemy icons and their respective HP bars. For the next part I am thinking about moving the buffs and debuffs under the HP bar as small icons too: if Magic Def down is applied, its effect is only visible during the application, but if the player still has it, it should be shown as a small icon under his HP bar.'*

This file is the design and the state. **Built: the HUD (this step). Designed, not built: the status icons under the bars (the next step, plan below).**

## What it looks like

```
 (portrait)=[ 120 / 120 =====bar=====>]  HERO  WARRIOR   VS   ♛ SLIME KING  BOSS  [<====bar===== 60 / 60 ]=(portrait)
              [status icons ...]                         STAGE 1                       [... status icons]
```
- **The two bars sit at the TOP of the stage** (they used to be a header row above it) and run in toward the **VS**. Each bar has a **round portrait** at its outer end: the portrait ring overlaps the bar's end like the reference; the hero's ring is gold on blue, the enemy's gold-bronze on red; **the boss's ring is thicker with a gold glow, its bar is taller and gold-framed, a crown comes before its name and the tag reads BOSS** (the other enemies' tag reads FOE, the hero's HERO).
- **Names:** the small tag sits next to the portrait and the name toward the centre, as in the reference (left: tag, then name, packed to the right; right: name, then tag, packed to the left).
- **The bar:** a pill with a silver rim, ten segment ticks (every 10%), a soft gloss on the top half, the number (120 / 120) in white at the portrait end. **The fill is anchored at the CENTRE and drains toward its portrait** (as the right bar of the reference). The hero's fill is lime green (the reference), the enemy's is red, the boss's red-orange. A **pale ghost bar** lags behind a hit (it shrinks 0.45 s later), the bar and the portrait **flash and shake on a hit**, the bar **pulses green on a heal**, **pulses (brighter) below 25%** and the **portrait goes grey when the fighter is defeated**.
- **VS:** an italic gold-to-orange VS with a dark outline and an orange glow, and **STAGE N** in small letters under it (it follows the stage; Reset makes it 1).
- **Portraits** are drawn from **frame 1 of each idle strip** (no new art): a square crop of the HEAD for the five heroes, of the WHOLE creature for the slimes (their faces are their bodies), the Elite without the sword and the King with his crown. The crops (`HUD_CROP` = [x, y, side] in the 512 cell): warrior [238, 51, 124], mage [228, 54, 124], rogue [243, 53, 124], assassin [253, 39, 124], spellblade [255, 44, 124]; fire slime [60, 100, 380], ice [85, 160, 350], lightning [60, 120, 385], dark [55, 95, 380], Elite [110, 160, 290], King [90, 100, 310] (measured on the strips and checked as circles). **The hero faces right (toward the VS) as drawn; the enemy is mirrored to face left, toward the VS.** The goblin placeholder has no picture: a green face is drawn (`hudPlaceholder`). The portrait follows the Character and Monster selects.
- **Sizes** come from the stage's width (`--hw`, set by a ResizeObserver): the portrait is 9.2% of the width (clamped 38 to 70 px; the boss 10.6%, 44 to 80 px), the bar 2.5% (12 to 19 px; the boss 3%, 14 to 23 px), the VS 6.2%. Checked at 760 px (the page) and 327 px (a phone: everything fits, the names stay readable).
- **Layers:** the HUD is above the characters, the effects and the Victory / Defeat banner (z-index 7), and the **stage banner ('Stage N') and the boss name banner moved down** (24% and 22% of the stage height) so they do not sit under the HUD.

## How it is wired (for the next person)
- The HUD elements are `#hpW` (hero) and `#hpG` (enemy) inside `#hud2` in the stage; the old header row is gone. **The code contract is unchanged:** each has a `.fill`, a `.v` (the number) and a `.name` whose first `span` is the name; `FIGHTERS.W.hpEl` and `FIGHTERS.G.hpEl` point to them. `hudPaint(f)` paints one bar (fill, ghost, number, states, the STAGE label); `paintHp(f)` = `hudPaint(f)` plus the monster's own hit and death reaction; `healBar(f)` (the King's heal) = `hudPaint(f)` without that reaction.
- `hudHero()` (called when the character changes and at the start) and `hudMonster()` (called by `setMonster`: portrait, FOE / BOSS tag, the boss frame) draw the portraits with `hudFace`.
- CSS: everything is under `.hud2` (classes `.hp`, `.hp-pt`, `.hp-body`, `.hp-ghost`, `.hp-tag`, `.hp-fx`, `.hud-vs`); the colours are three gradients (`#hpW .fill`, `#hpG .fill`, `#hpG.boss .fill`) and a few numbers (the ring, the ticks): all single values.
- **Left for the status icons: an empty row `.hp-fx` (`#fxW`, `#fxG`) under each bar**, at least 1.3 bar-heights tall, the icons packed from the portrait end (left bar: left; right bar: right, as the reference). Tested with four 18 px chips on a 327 px phone width and the 760 px page: they fit under the bar without touching the stage.

## The status icons: the design for the next step (not built)
- **The rule the user gave:** the effect when a status is APPLIED stays exactly as it is now (the burst on the target, the flash, the 'MAGIC DEF DOWN' style number); **while the status lasts, a small icon sits under that fighter's HP bar**, and it goes when the status ends.
- **One registry** `STATUSES = { id: { name, kind: "buff" | "debuff", glyph, colour } }` for the statuses the page already has: **Burn** (stacks, turns), **Chilled**, **Frozen**, **Shocked**, **Magic Def down** (Ward), **Elusive** (the Rogue's buff), **Guard** (the Elite's Stone Guard), **Defense up**; each apply and clear function the page already has (`applyBurn`/`clearBurn`, `applyChilled`/`clearChilled`, `applyShocked`/`clearShocked`, `applyWard`/`clearWard`, `applyElusive`/`clearElusive`, `applyGuard`/`clearGuard`, and the tick that counts turns down) calls one `hudStatus(fighter, id, { turns, stacks })` / `hudStatusClear(fighter, id)`: no new game logic, only the display.
- **The chip:** a rounded square, about 1.3 bar-heights, with a code-drawn glyph (a flame, a snowflake, a bolt, a broken shield, a cloak, a shield, an up arrow) in the status's colour; a **red frame for a debuff, a green-gold frame for a buff**; a tiny **turns number** in the corner (and **xN** for stacks); a short pop when it appears and a fade when it ends; the row wraps after about six. A tap or hover shows the name ('Magic Def down, 2 turns') for the tester.
- **Recommended:** code-drawn glyph chips first (consistent, no art to wait for); painted icons can replace the glyphs later (the elemental pieces already exist in `source/assets/fx`).
- **Questions for the user:** which statuses to show first (the list above is every one the page has); whether the chip should show the turns left; whether buffs go on the same row or a second row.

## Round 2 (2026-11-12, the user's four notes before the status icons)

3. **The boss's bar aligns with the hero's.** The boss had a bigger portrait and a taller bar and a bigger name pushing its bar down. ## The status icons are BUILT (2026-11-12, round 3) and supersede the plan above

**The user's answers:** (1) *which statuses first:* no fixed order: **the icons appear in the order the statuses are applied**; (2) *turns left:* **not shown**; **stacks ARE shown**: a small, readable number at the bottom right of the icon (Burn is stackable); (3) *the layout:* **the HP bar has 10 ticks: under ticks 1 to 5 the BUFFS, under ticks 6 to 10 the DEBUFFS; buffs start at the left and grow to the right as more are applied; debuffs start at the very end of the bar and grow toward the left**; (4) *who designs the icons:* the user asked; **I drew them in code (inline SVG), a painted pass stays optional** (below).
- **Layout:** the row under each bar is split in two halves of five slots, one slot per tick (`--sl` = the bar's width / 10, set by the script and kept up to date by a ResizeObserver); an icon is centred in its slot and about 1.6 bar heights big (`--ic`, never wider than the slot): 21 px on the 760 px page. **Hero (left bar):** buffs fill slots 1 to 5 from the portrait end, debuffs fill slots 10 down to 6 from the VS end. **Foe (right bar) is MIRRORED** (`HUD_FX_MIRROR = true`): its buffs start at ITS portrait end (the right) and grow left, its debuffs start at the VS end (the left end of its bar) and grow right; set it to `false` to make the right bar read exactly like the left one. At most five icons show in each group; a sixth shows as a small red '+N' on the fifth (no status combination reaches it yet: there are five debuffs and two buffs).
- **The look:** a rounded square with a coloured frame: a **red frame for a debuff**, a **gold frame on a green-black ground for a buff**; the stack number is white with a black outline at the bottom right of the icon (shown for a stackable status once it has 2 or more stacks: Burn x2, x3). A new icon pops in (a flash and a settle), a Burn that gains a stack bumps, an icon whose status ends shrinks and fades; hovering an icon shows its name ('Burn x2'). `prefers-reduced-motion` turns the animations off.
- **The glyphs (`HUD_STATUS`, drawn as SVG, outlined and flat-coloured to stay readable at 18 to 24 px; checked enlarged to 84 px):** **Burn** a flame (orange with a yellow heart), **Chilled** a snowflake (pale blue), **Frozen** an ice block (a blue isometric cube), **Shocked** a lightning bolt (yellow), **Magic Def down** a violet shield with a white crack, **Elusive** a wind swirl (pale violet), **Defense up** a steel shield with a gold chevron up. Adding a status = one entry in `HUD_STATUS` (name, kind, svg, `stack: true` when it stacks) and one name in `HUD_ORDER` plus the fighter field it reads.
- **How it works (no game code was changed for it):** `hudFxSync` runs ten times a second and reads the statuses the page already keeps on each fighter (`burn {stacks}`, `chilled`, `frozen`, `shocked`, `magDef`, `elusive`, `guard`: set by the existing apply functions, cleared by the clear functions, the turn ticks and Reset); a new field value adds an icon at the end of its group, a cleared one removes it. So the icons are always true to the game state and follow the applied order, whatever applies the status (a monster's spit, the Rogue, the Mage, a boss skill).
- **A small bug fixed on the way:** `resetState` did not clear **Elusive** (both fighters) or the hero's **Defense up**: after a Reset the page still thought the Rogue was Elusive (and a new Elusive would not draw its effect). It clears them now.
- **The test panel 'Status icons: try them on the stage'** (under the stage controls): pick the hero or the foe and press Burn (+1 stack), Chilled, Frozen, Shocked, Magic Def down, Elusive, Defense up or Clear all: it applies the REAL statuses (the same effects and icons as in a fight). **Checked in the browser pane:** every status on both bars, the applied order (hero: Elusive then Defense up from the left, Burn x2, Shocked, Magic Def, Chilled, Frozen from the right end), the foe mirrored (its Elusive and Defense up at its portrait end, its Chilled, Burn x3 and Shocked growing from the VS end), the stack numbers, Clear all, Reset, a real fight against the fire slime (the burn icon appears on the hero), a full fight to the next stage, the King's bar: no console errors.
- **Not done / for later:** the Mage's **Barrier** is a passive glass for the whole fight, not a status (no icon yet: could be a buff icon); the other statuses the game will have (Stun, Poison, Bleed, Attack up and so on) need a glyph each; **painted icons** can replace the glyphs: a 4 x 2 sheet of eight icons on magenta (a prompt can be written: a square badge each, the status's symbol, thick dark outline, no frame since the page draws the frame), cut like the other pieces; on a phone (327 px) the icons are 13 px (the stack number is hard to read there: the phone layout is parked).

**Decided (the user, 2026-11-12): KEEP the right bar MIRRORED** (its buffs at its portrait end, its debuffs from the VS end); `HUD_FX_MIRROR = true` stays. Do not change without the user's word.

## Round 4 (2026-11-12, the user found a bug and asked for two changes while trying the statuses)

**TODO LATER (the user, 'take note'):** **an 8-icon painted sheet to replace the drawn glyphs** (4 x 2 on magenta, the status's symbol only, the page draws the frame; prompt not written yet) and the **block animations** (`docs/BLOCK.md`).

1. **Bug, fixed: a monster's Elusive was drawn round the HERO.** The user: 'when I choose Elusive for the monster the icon is under the monster's bar but the effect appears round the player'. Yes, it was a bug: `elusiveDraw` always used the hero's geometry (`bsFig()`) and the hero's sprite. The layer now remembers who has it (`L.A`) and a monster's Elusive is drawn on the monster's figure (`fzFig`). Checked in the pane: with the Elite as foe, the wisps and the ground pool are inside the Elite's sprite and lower than the hero's (the paint's bounding boxes: hero 79-178 x 299-454, foe 592-691 x 335-454), the sprite of each is darkened (`brightness(0.84)`), no console errors.
2. **Frozen (and every status that uses `fzGeom`) follows the FIGURE, not the unit's box.** The user: 'the Frozen animation has a fixed size, built for the placeholder goblin dimensions; for the characters the size is OK but it is not centred'. Before, the block, the aura and the shatter were sized by the target's unit box (the same for every monster, centred on the unit: a character stands 7% to the right of it and the King is twice as wide as the box). Now `fzFig(T)` measures the opaque bounding box of the fighter's idle frame once (`figFrac`, cached on the picture) and places it on the stage through the frame's rectangle on screen, so it follows the **Monster size slider, the mirror (`flip`) and the stage width**. `fzGeom` returns the block's size as the figure times `FZ_FIT` (1.27 wide, 1.36 tall: exactly the hero's old block, so the characters keep their size and are now centred), the equivalent unit `uEq` (ring, glints and mist sizes are shares of it, so they scale too), and an equivalent unit box for the effects that read `g.r` (Chilled, Magic Def down, Burn, the Rogue's Permafrost Execution). **Checked in the pane:** hero and fire slime frozen (each centred on its figure), the Elite (a low wide block, not the tall goblin box), the King (a big block that covers him), the fire slime at 140% (the block grew with it), Burn, Chilled, Magic Def down and Shocked on both fighters: no console errors. The goblin placeholder (no picture) keeps the old unit-box geometry as a fallback.
3. **Frozen = STILL.** The user: 'any character during the frozen state should stop animating until the effect passes: not even the idle animation'. From `holdAt` (55% of the way into the freeze's arrival, when the ice has closed over most of the body) until the block breaks, the fighter's picture is not redrawn: the hero's `showFrame` and the monster's idle (`monStartIdle`) and pack (`monPlayPack`) frames return at once (`heldFrozen(T)`), so it stands on the frame it was on. A fighter with 0 hit points is released at once (its death pack starts at the impact, before the block is thawed). **Checked:** hero and foe frozen: their `backgroundPosition` stayed on ONE frame for 8 samples; after Clear all they went back to cycling six frames. Not seen in a real fight turn (a frozen monster's lost turn, a lance's shatter): the hold ends at the same places the block does (`thaw`, `clearStatuses`, a death).
