# The in-stage HUD (2026-11-12)

The user (2026-11-12), with a reference picture of a versus screen from another game (a round portrait at each end, the HP bars running in to a big **VS** in the middle, a name above each bar, a caption under it):
*'Can you design HP bars to be shown in the stage like that? Player/enemy icons and their respective HP bars. For the next part I am thinking about moving the buffs and debuffs under the HP bar as small icons too: if Magic Def down is applied, its effect is only visible during the application, but if the player still has it, it should be shown as a small icon under his HP bar.'*

This file is the design and the state. **Built: the HUD (this step). Designed, not built: the status icons under the bars (the next step, plan below).**

## What it looks like

```
 (portrait)=[ 120 / 120 =====bar=====>]  HERO  WARRIOR   VS   ♛ SLIME KING  BOSS  [<====bar===== 60 / 60 ]=(portrait)
              [status icons ...]                         STAGE 1                       [... status icons]
```
- **The two bars sit at the TOP of the stage** (they used to be a header row above it) and run in toward the **VS**. Each bar has a **round portrait** at its outer end: the portrait ring overlaps the bar's end like the reference; the hero's ring is gold on blue, the enemy's gold-bronze on red; **the boss's ring is thicker with a gold glow, its bar is taller and gold-framed, a crown comes before its name and the tag reads BOSS** (the other enemies' tag reads FOE, the hero's HERO).
- **Names:** the small tag sits next to the portrait and the name toward the centre, as in the reference (left: tag, then name, packed to the right; right: name, then tag, packed to the left).
- **The bar:** a pill with a silver rim, ten segment ticks (every 10%), a soft gloss on the top half, the number (120 / 120) in white at the portrait end. **The fill is anchored at the CENTRE and drains toward its portrait** (as the right bar of the reference). The hero's fill is lime green (the reference), the enemy's is red, the boss's red-orange. A **pale ghost bar** lags behind a hit (it shrinks 0.45 s later), the bar and the portrait **flash and shake on a hit**, the bar **pulses green on a heal**, **pulses (brighter) below 25%** and the **portrait goes grey when the fighter is defeated**.
- **VS:** an italic gold-to-orange VS with a dark outline and an orange glow, and **STAGE N** in small letters under it (it follows the stage; Reset makes it 1).
- **Portraits** are drawn from **frame 1 of each idle strip** (no new art): a square crop of the HEAD for the five heroes, of the WHOLE creature for the slimes (their faces are their bodies), the Elite without the sword and the King with his crown. The crops (`HUD_CROP` = [x, y, side] in the 512 cell): warrior [238, 51, 124], mage [228, 54, 124], rogue [243, 53, 124], assassin [253, 39, 124], spellblade [255, 44, 124]; fire slime [60, 100, 380], ice [85, 160, 350], lightning [60, 120, 385], dark [55, 95, 380], Elite [110, 160, 290], King [90, 100, 310] (measured on the strips and checked as circles). **The hero faces right (toward the VS) as drawn; the enemy is mirrored to face left, toward the VS.** The goblin placeholder has no picture: a green face is drawn (`hudPlaceholder`). The portrait follows the Character and Monster selects.
- **Sizes** come from the stage's width (`--hw`, set by a ResizeObserver): the portrait is 9.2% of the width (clamped 38 to 70 px; the boss 10.6%, 44 to 80 px), the bar 2.5% (12 to 19 px; the boss 3%, 14 to 23 px), the VS 6.2%. Checked at 760 px (the page) and 327 px (a phone: everything fits, the names stay readable).
- **Layers:** the HUD is above the characters, the effects and the Victory / Defeat banner (z-index 7), and the **stage banner ('Stage N') and the boss name banner moved down** (24% and 22% of the stage height) so they do not sit under the HUD.

## How it is wired (for the next person)
- The HUD elements are `#hpW` (hero) and `#hpG` (enemy) inside `#hud2` in the stage; the old header row is gone. **The code contract is unchanged:** each has a `.fill`, a `.v` (the number) and a `.name` whose first `span` is the name; `FIGHTERS.W.hpEl` and `FIGHTERS.G.hpEl` point to them. `hudPaint(f)` paints one bar (fill, ghost, number, states, the STAGE label); `paintHp(f)` = `hudPaint(f)` plus the monster's own hit and death reaction; `healBar(f)` (the King's heal) = `hudPaint(f)` without that reaction.
- `hudHero()` (called when the character changes and at the start) and `hudMonster()` (called by `setMonster`: portrait, FOE / BOSS tag, the boss frame) draw the portraits with `hudFace`.
- CSS: everything is under `.hud2` (classes `.hp`, `.hp-pt`, `.hp-body`, `.hp-ghost`, `.hp-tag`, `.hp-fx`, `.hud-vs`); the colours are three gradients (`#hpW .fill`, `#hpG .fill`, `#hpG.boss .fill`) and a few numbers (the ring, the ticks): all single values.
- **Left for the status icons: an empty row `.hp-fx` (`#fxW`, `#fxG`) under each bar**, at least 1.3 bar-heights tall, the icons packed from the portrait end (left bar: left; right bar: right, as the reference). Tested with four 18 px chips on a 327 px phone width and the 760 px page: they fit under the bar without touching the stage.

## The status icons: the design for the next step (not built)
- **The rule the user gave:** the effect when a status is APPLIED stays exactly as it is now (the burst on the target, the flash, the 'MAGIC DEF DOWN' style number); **while the status lasts, a small icon sits under that fighter's HP bar**, and it goes when the status ends.
- **One registry** `STATUSES = { id: { name, kind: "buff" | "debuff", glyph, colour } }` for the statuses the page already has: **Burn** (stacks, turns), **Chilled**, **Frozen**, **Shocked**, **Magic Def down** (Ward), **Elusive** (the Rogue's buff), **Guard** (the Elite's Stone Guard), **Defense up**; each apply and clear function the page already has (`applyBurn`/`clearBurn`, `applyChilled`/`clearChilled`, `applyShocked`/`clearShocked`, `applyWard`/`clearWard`, `applyElusive`/`clearElusive`, `applyGuard`/`clearGuard`, and the tick that counts turns down) calls one `hudStatus(fighter, id, { turns, stacks })` / `hudStatusClear(fighter, id)`: no new game logic, only the display.
- **The chip:** a rounded square, about 1.3 bar-heights, with a code-drawn glyph (a flame, a snowflake, a bolt, a broken shield, a cloak, a shield, an up arrow) in the status's colour; a **red frame for a debuff, a green-gold frame for a buff**; a tiny **turns number** in the corner (and **xN** for stacks); a short pop when it appears and a fade when it ends; the row wraps after about six. A tap or hover shows the name ('Magic Def down, 2 turns') for the tester.
- **Recommended:** code-drawn glyph chips first (consistent, no art to wait for); painted icons can replace the glyphs later (the elemental pieces already exist in `source/assets/fx`).
- **Questions for the user:** which statuses to show first (the list above is every one the page has); whether the chip should show the turns left; whether buffs go on the same row or a second row.

## Round 2 (2026-11-12, the user's four notes before the status icons)
1. **70% size.** 'A bit big for the stage layout: reduce to 70%.' Every HUD size is now 0.7 of the first version: the portrait 6.4% of the stage's width (clamped 28 to 50 px; it was 38 to 70), the bar 1.75% (9 to 14 px), the names 9 to 11 px, the VS 16 to 32 px, the paddings. At the 760 px page the portrait is 49 px and the bar 13 px; on a 327 px phone the portrait is 28 px and the bar 9 px (checked: readable).
2. **The bar starts right next to the portrait.** The bar was tucked under the ring (a negative margin), so its first tick showed inside the icon's area. Now the bar's rounded end starts just outside the ring (`margin = .1 x the portrait + 2 px`: the ring reaches .09 of the portrait beyond it, so about 2 to 3 px of air), the number sits inside the bar by .6 of its height, and the status row starts at the same edge. Checked at 760 and 327 px.
3. **The boss's bar aligns with the hero's.** The boss had a bigger portrait and a taller bar and a bigger name pushing its bar down. Now **the portrait size and the bar size are the SAME for both** (the boss's ring is only bolder: a thicker gold ring with a glow, which does not change the layout), and **the name row has ONE fixed height (`--nh`, 13 to 17 px) for both**, the names bottom-aligned in it: the boss keeps a **bigger, golden name with a crown and a gold BOSS tag**, and its bar sits exactly where the hero's does (measured: both tracks 143 px from the top, 13 px high; on a phone both 151 px, 9 px).
4. **The Slime King is back at the right edge** (see `docs/SLIME_KING.md`).
The stage banner and the boss-name banner moved back up under the smaller HUD (19% and 17% of the stage's height). The row under each bar for the status icons is unchanged (it starts at the bar's start, next to the portrait).
