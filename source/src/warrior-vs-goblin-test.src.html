Status icons: try them on the stage
About this Made 2026-11-12 (docs/HUD.md). While a status lasts it shows as a small icon under its fighter's HP bar: buffs (Elusive, Defense up) fill the first five ticks of the bar, the first applied nearest the portrait; debuffs (Burn, Chilled, Frozen, Shocked, Magic Def down) fill the other five ticks starting from the end of the bar, toward the VS, and grow toward the middle as more are applied; Burn shows its stack count (2 or 3) at the bottom right. These buttons apply the real statuses (the same effects, the same icons the fights use); in a fight the icons come and go with the statuses by themselves. Hover an icon for its name.
Treasure look: the Slime King's hoard in the stage
About this Made 2026-11-12 so the look of the treasure can be set by eye (the user: the haze and the blur were too strong). It changes only how the hoard's picture is drawn behind the Slime King, never the art file, and it works at once (move a slider and look). Blur softens the crisp outlines (0 to 3 px), Haze veils the picture with the far forest's green (it recedes), Colour and Brightness are as for the fighters, Forest tint tints it toward the forest, Black lift turns the dark outline toward the forest's shadow green, Soft base fades the bottom rows so the coins melt into the ground. Size is the hoard's width against the King's and Stands back how far up the stage it stands. Your settings are remembered in this browser; Copy my values puts them in the box so they can be pasted into a message and made the defaults.

Numbers for the game (JSON)
About this Drawn from code, like the lightning, and tied to the frames of the cast. One cast animation serves every skill. While the cast charges, the orb glows in the skill's colour and the top of the staff reacts (both follow the hand marks). Firebolt: a fire head fades in at the top of the staff (horizontal, pointing at the target), on the release frame the bolt leaves the top of the staff, flies in a slight arc to the target and bursts on it. Ice Shard (and Deep Freeze, and Shatter Lance with its own levels): a frost aura swirls round the mage on the first three frames. Ice Shard: an ice crystal fades in just in front of the top of the staff (horizontal, pointing at the target), is thrown on the release frame, flies fast and almost straight, turning about its axis, and shatters on the target (pieces, frost spikes from the ground, mist). Deep Freeze: no projectile; a line of frost creeps along the ground to the target, an ice aura slowly closes in and ice climbs its body until it is sealed in a giant block; when it freezes the target (the "Freeze chance"), the block is held for the target's whole next turn, which it loses, and then shatters; when the target resists, the ice breaks part-way. Shatter Lance: in four beats on four frames of the cast, a small ice shard fades in at a line you mark on the stage (50%, then 100%), the lance forms out of it, is fired at once with cold mist behind it, and strikes with a starburst and spires bursting from the ground; on a Chilled target it is a guaranteed critical hit, on a Frozen one it breaks the block (+100% damage) and leaves a cracked ward (Magic Def -25% for 2 turns). Ice Shard chills its target (tick the box), so a fight can show all three cases; or pick the target in the preview. Cinderburst: a compact ball of molten fire forms at the top of the staff while embers are drawn into it, is thrown at once and bursts in a big explosion; Firebolt sets its target ablaze (Burn, up to 3 stacks, tick the box) and when Cinderburst hits a burning target every burn pops first, in a quick chain, and the blast deals what is left of the burns (or pick the target in the preview). Pyroclasm: no projectile; a vortex of fire forms at the top of the staff and falls into the ground, a crack of lava runs along the ground to the target and a towering inferno erupts there, with a pyroclastic cloud and an ember storm; heavy damage and 3 Burn stacks at once. The box holds the numbers for the chosen skill (skill-fx/1). Play a fight with the mage, or use the preview (it switches to the mage by itself).

Passive: Barrier (the mage)

Numbers for the game (JSON)
About this The mage's passive, drawn from code (no pictures). When combat starts she casts a plain, round glass bubble round herself: it is raised from the ground with a ring of light climbing it, ends in a flash and a ring of light over the ground, and then stays up at the chosen Visibility (half by default) until the fight ends, with a soft sheen sweeping over it now and then. Background: the Forest Kingdom
About this PvE stages 1 to 100 are the Forest Kingdom, in five acts of 20 stages: Glimmerwood (slimes), Mistwood Hollow (goblins), Moonshade Woods (wolves), Ashfall Reach (ogres) and Warhorn Fields (orcs). Each act has its own pictures in source/assets/bg/<act>/: far (the forest wall), ground (the floor), props (objects standing on the far half of the floor) and, if there is one, mid; made by make_bg_layer.py. All five acts have their real pictures (far, ground and props, generated by the user); an act without pictures would show the nearest act that has them. They slide while the run plays, far 25%, mid 60%, props 85% and the ground 100% of the ground's speed (the front-most picture there is moves at 100%). The ground's speed is tied to the run's frames (so it follows the Run fps box and the speed buttons) and set per character: each run strip has a different stride (the warrior takes one step per loop, the mage two), so each character has its own number, remembered in this browser. The scroll eases in and out. Reset, Play or any other animation stops it. After a win the run goes on for 4 seconds, then "Stage 2" (3, 4, ...) appears, the ground and the run slow to a stop together and a new monster rides in and fades in; when the stage enters the next act (21, 41, 61, 81) the look cross-fades into the new act's and the caption names the act. Play fights the new monster; Reset goes back to Stage 1. Type a stage number to jump (try 20, then win, or 40). The Look select also has the Arena (PvP): a fixed colosseum view with no scroll, no run across the world and no stage change, so a win only shows Victory and Reset starts the fight again (the Stage box is off there). Its pictures (far, ground and props in source/assets/bg/arena/, prompts in docs/PROMPTS.md) are the user's, made 2026-11-03; its far picture has its wall base at 75.8% of the height, set in horizon.txt, and its props are one fixed set of five (cold fire basket, armour stand, gong, armour stand, cold fire basket, equal gaps, the gong at the exact centre of the stage), drawn once and centred (centered.txt). The dungeon, Rootdeep Crypt, has four looks after the arena in the Look select: Crypt Halls (the regular stages: scrolls and changes stages like an act but keeps its look) and three fixed views drawn once and centred, Rootgate (the entrance), Vigil Hall (the rest area) and Throne of Roots (the boss room); each folder's horizon.txt says where its wall meets the floor (and its floor's far edge). With the Rootgate chosen, the button Enter the dungeon (preview) shows the entrance walk with the one character the page has (the game will use the party of four): it runs to the open gate at the ground's pace while the picture stays still, goes in through the door (up into the passage, smaller, fading) and the stage cross-fades into the Crypt Halls; pick the Rootgate again to repeat it. The gate's place is in the scene folder's gate.txt.
Stage blend: the fighters in the scene
About this Made 2026-11-07 because the fighters, and the fire slime most of all, looked like stickers on the painted forest (docs/MONSTERS.md and docs/blend_test_1.png). It changes only how the two fighters are drawn, never the art files, and the skill effects are not touched. Colour is the saturation of each fighter (the monster has its own, because the slime is the most saturated thing on the stage), Black lift pulls the pure blacks of the outlines toward the forest's dark green-brown (the stage has no black), Forest tint multiplies the fighters by the light colour of the forest, Brightness darkens or lightens them. Ground shadow is a soft dark oval on the ground under each fighter that stays on the ground when the fighter jumps and fades with the height (the hard black oval of the first version was removed on 2026-11-02; this one is soft and you choose how strong). Sunbeams lay two soft diagonal shafts of light over the whole stage, in front of the fighters. Softness blurs the fighters (px x 0.1): it is here so you can see what it costs (the faces smear); the recommendation is 0. The settings are remembered in this browser. All of it is a colour matrix and a few overlays, so the game can do the same with a Pixi colour-matrix filter.

    Developer tools: frame paths, your own images, animation packs and fps, hand marks (folded away: the animations load on their own)

    Frame paths: where each frame stands

    Load an attack pack to edit its path.
    Pick a frame with the numbers, then drag it on the stage (arrow keys nudge 1 px, with Shift 10 px; , and . switch frames). X 0 is the home position, 100 is the strike point in front of the target; go past 100 or below 0 if you want. Lift is how far the whole frame is raised, on top of the height already drawn in it (100 = one character width). Nothing can go below the ground line, shown as a dashed gold line while editing. The red-edged frame is the hit frame (for the dodge pack: the frame where the strike would have landed). For the dodge pack X is in character widths from the home position, negative = away from the enemy. The box above holds the numbers for the game: copy it or download it.
