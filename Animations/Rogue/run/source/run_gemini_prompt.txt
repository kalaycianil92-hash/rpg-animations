ROGUE RUN PROMPT (6 different poses of ONE running step, 3 x 2). Written 2026-11-04. Works with Gemini or ChatGPT.

WHY THIS FORM (docs/PROMPTS.md, "Run in place"): image generators cannot draw the leg swap of a second step; they repeat the first step, or turn the torso away from the camera. What worked for the warrior (the "step6" prompt, `Animations/Warrior/run/source/run_gemini_prompt_step6.txt`) is to ask for SIX DIFFERENT poses of ONE step with the position of the boot on the floor in percent of the height, so that it slides steadily backward from panel to panel, plus a wide diagonal arm swing in three of the panels. This is that prompt with her blocks. The loop is then one step (five of the six panels, 9 fps), as the warrior's.

- What to look at: (1) six DIFFERENT poses, not one pose repeated; (2) the boot on the floor slides backward from panel 1 to 5 (about +22, +12, +2, -8, -25% of her height from the hips); (3) the torso faces the camera side-on in every panel (not turned away); (4) panels 1, 2 and 5 have the wide diagonal arm swing, one fist forward and up, the other back and down (the back fist is where her dagger will be); (5) the same woman and the same size in every panel (burgundy bob, crimson scarf, charcoal cloak, no blue), nothing cropped, nothing touching a panel edge (the cloak streams back and needs room); (6) empty closed fists; (7) one image only.

IMPORTANT: this script defaults to the strict despill (10 and 4), which turns her burgundy hair black at the outline (see PLAYBOOK.md); always pass `--despill-band 3 --despill-thr 12`. The head scale comes from a template match of her idle's head (the figure ends up about 86% of the idle's height because a run is drawn with a bigger head and bent knees). Her idle's head x is to be measured (the warrior's is 288). File it as `Animations/Rogue/run/cloaked_run_5frame_strip.png`, check it with `check_strip.py`, add a `.placement.json` with only the fps (9) like the warrior's, rebuild the page.
