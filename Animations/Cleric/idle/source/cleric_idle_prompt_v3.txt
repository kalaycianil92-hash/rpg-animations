The differences between the six panels are TINY: someone looking at them must have to look hard to see them.

CHARACTER: the exact same character as in the attached reference image: an adult elven man, a cleric-knight (a warrior-priest, a steadfast defender), about thirty-five in appearance, with a tall, solid, broad-shouldered, heavy-set frame, warm light tan skin with a slight olive undertone, long slender pointed elf ears visible at the side of his head, a handsome, strong, serious face with high cheekbones, a straight nose and a firm jaw, no beard and no moustache, large, open, bright steel-blue eyes and strong straight emerald-green eyebrows, and a calm, steady, resolute expression; short, neat, swept-back emerald-green hair (a clean mid-green) that ends above the collar and leaves the ear fully visible; a complete set of polished silver-steel plate armour: a steel gorget over a steel-grey ring-mail collar, big rounded steel pauldrons with a thin gold edge, a steel-grey ring-mail hauberk whose short sleeves show between the pauldrons and the elbow plates, steel elbow plates and plain steel vambraces (no gold) covering the forearms up to the wrists, a steel cuirass under a sleeveless cream tabard (surcoat) with a front and a back panel hanging to mid-thigh, open at the sides, with a thin gold edge and a gold embroidered radiant cross emblem with small rays on the chest; a dark-brown leather belt with a plain gold buckle and nothing hanging from it; bare hands; fitted dark-brown leather trousers; steel knee plates with a thin gold edge; steel greaves on the shins; knee-high dark-brown leather boots with a gold-edged cuff and flat soles; no cape, no cloak, no hood, no helmet, no gauntlets, no scarf, no pouch. VIEW: strict side view of the body and legs, he faces RIGHT in every panel, with the head turned a little toward the viewer in the same three-quarter view as in the reference. The enemy is off-screen to the right. The camera angle is the same in all six panels.

BOTH FEET AND BOTH BOOTS STAY PLANTED on exactly the same spot in every panel, flat on the floor, in the same wide stance as in the reference; they never slide, lift or hop. THE SIX FRAMES:

2. Breath begins. The chest and the shoulders rise by a hair (about 0.5% of the panel height), the head rises the same amount. Everything else is exactly as in panel 1.

5. Breath out. The chest and the shoulders come down to about 0.5% BELOW their height in panel 1, the head lowers by about 0.5%, the knees give a hair. The hem of the tabard swings slightly forward. The feet and the fists do not move.
6. Settle. The chest and the shoulders rise back almost to their height in panel 1. This is the pose just before panel 1: nearly identical to it, with the hem of the tabard a hair behind. It must not be an exact copy of panel 1.

BACKGROUND AND LAYOUT: one flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) background filling the whole image, identical in every pixel. No gradient, floor, ground line, cast shadow, glow or vignette. No grid lines or borders between panels. Leave about 10% empty margin around the figure in each panel; the whole character, with the hands and the feet, must stay fully inside its own panel and never touch or cross a panel edge.
