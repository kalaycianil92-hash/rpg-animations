THE SKILL (the game's text, InfinityTower `src/App.jsx` c_a5, 2 cooldown, holy, dmg x1.40, the heaviest of the six): "Calls down a pillar of divine light for heavy Holy Damage, dealing +40% bonus damage if the target is Purified or has Reduce Magic Def. If this lands while the Cleric has an active reflect barrier, cleanses 1 random debuff from the Cleric." (The bonus, the cleanse, Purified and Reduce Magic Def are NOT drawn: all buffs and debuffs come later. The numbers are the engine's business.)

THE LOOK (my design). The first four skills are: Radiant Smite = high, bright, advancing (an overhead stroke and a lunge); Dark Penance = low, cold, still (a kneel, the head BOWED); Aegis of Retribution = a braced cast that draws a shield; Unholy Siphon = a reach and a wrench. Sanctified Judgment is the HEAVY one, the verdict, and it comes FROM ABOVE: he does not strike the enemy, he calls the light down on it. So it is SOLEMN and SLOW: he stands where he stands, lifts his head to the sky with his eyes closed as if listening to a voice (the opposite of Dark Penance's bowed head), raises the weapon arm straight up while rising onto his toes with a ringing cry and HOLDS it there for a long moment (the page draws the ring of light on the ground under the target and the light gathering above it), then THROWS the arm down and points it at the enemy in one verdict (the pillar of light crashes down on the target), and holds the pointing arm while the pillar burns. It works with the MACE (the Templar) and with the WAND (the Occultist): a raised arm and a pointing arm, nothing that only a club can do.
