WHY THESE CHOICES (my decisions, change any of them before generating if you like)
- DEEP, BLUISH violet, never neon or plum: the cutters key the magenta background by "red and blue both well above green". A neon or electric violet, and a plum violet where red is about as strong as blue, looks like magenta to the key and is partly cut away (a bright violet such as (150, 50, 200) keeps only 73% of its opacity, (160, 32, 240) only 47%). Measured inside the figures (not counting the anti-aliased edge): ChatGPT's first attempt 2.1% partly see-through (the scarf), the muscular version 1.8% (mostly the cape lining, about (126, 39, 135): 85% opaque), almost none fully lost. Deep amethyst, aubergine and dusty lavender with blue stronger than red (all measured: 100% kept) are safe. No pink, no magenta anywhere.
- Silver-white hair and nothing else white or silver (dark gunmetal for metal): the hair stays the one easy-to-select part (see the hair-mask note in docs/DEV_NOTES.md); the white-haired mood picture and ChatGPT's attempt both point the same way.
- Short, sleek hair (jaw length, longer on the viewer's side): long flowing hair would be a second moving element in every frame and the drift between frames would grow.
- BARE hands (no gloves): the page draws the weapon's grip and then the hand's own pixels over it, found by the hand's skin colour around the hand mark; a gloved fist has no skin and falls back to a rough circle. Bracers on the forearms are fine.
- The body stays in strict side view (every animation is drawn that way); the neckline reads as a V on the visible side, the cleavage is a hint. The mask hides the mouth, so shouts and grimaces in the hit and death frames are carried by the eyes, which also makes the frames easier to redraw consistently.
- Same height and head size as the Rogue so the existing poses, marks and paths fit. "Stronger" is meant as a strong presence (the stance, the gaze, the sculpted armour), NOT as muscles: the first version of this prompt asked for "broad strong shoulders, defined muscular upper arms, strong shapely thighs" and the generator drew a muscular body (look_v1_muscular.png); the words are gone, the prompt now asks for a lean dancer's or fencer's body.
