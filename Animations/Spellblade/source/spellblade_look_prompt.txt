WHY THESE CHOICES (my decisions, change any of them before generating if you like)
- Scarlet-red hair and NO other red on her: the pilot plan wants a hair colour nothing else on the figure shares (the hair is the one easy-to-select part, see the hair-mask note in docs/DEV_NOTES.md). So the cape lining is pale gold, the clasp gem pale amber, the eyes amber: no red gem, no red lining, no red lips beyond a natural tone. The red is an orange-leaning SCARLET (#D2321E to #E04A2A), never pink, crimson-magenta or burgundy: the cutters key the magenta background by "red and blue both well above green", a scarlet with almost no blue is safe, a pink-red or a violet-red would be partly cut away.
- Ivory, not pure white: a warm ivory (#F1E9D8) with soft warm shadows (#CFC2A6) reads as a painted fabric and cannot be mistaken for the key. Gold is a warm, slightly deep gold (#C99A2E to #E8C060), not greenish, not yellow-neon.
- BARE hands (no gloves): the page draws the weapon's grip and then the hand's own pixels over it, found by the hand's skin colour around the hand mark; a gloved fist has no skin and falls back to a rough circle. So the sleeves end at the wrists, with gold bracers over them. The skin is a warm honey-tan, clearly darker than the ivory of the suit, so the hand and the waist skin are easy to tell from the clothes.
- Short hair (jaw length): long flowing hair would be a second moving element in every frame and the drift between frames would grow. It is a polished, layered bob (the Assassin's is a ragged sleek one), so the two branches differ in silhouette.
- The waist cut-out is drawn as a band of bare skin on the visible side of the waist (every animation is strict side view); the bodysuit's chest piece and hip piece are joined by thin straps and a small gold ring, nothing wraps the waist, so it cannot turn into a belt.
- NO skirt: you described a bodysuit with covered legs, so the hips are bare of any skirt or tabard (see the edit prompt at the bottom to add a short tabard).
- Same height and head size as the Rogue and the Assassin so the existing poses, marks and paths fit. "Strong" is NOT asked for as muscles: the Assassin's first prompt asked for "strong shoulders, defined arms" and the generator drew a muscular body. The body is a lean dancer's or fencer's, and with covered limbs the shape shows through fitted fabric.
- Weapons stay the page's cutlass and dagger layers (the pictures show bare fists). The holy / elemental hint is only in the design (gold filigree line-work, the gem clasp), never as glow or effects in the picture.
