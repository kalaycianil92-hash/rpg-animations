WARRIOR: BLOCK (6 frames, 3 x 2). Written 2026-11-10. The first Block sheet of the game: the Warrior is the pilot, the other classes copy its pattern (docs/BLOCK.md).

WHAT IT IS. A block is a REACTION, like the Dodge: when a hit lands, it may be blocked, and this animation plays for about half a second INSTEAD of the Hit animation. The guard goes up BEFORE the blow lands (frame 3), the blow lands on frame 4 (the page puts the glass of the Barrier there and cuts the damage by 20%), the guard eases (frame 5) and he is back in his guard stance (frame 6). He stays on the same spot: only the arms, the knees and the upper body move.

THE WEAPONS ARE A LAYER (as in every Warrior sheet): his hands are drawn as empty, tight fists and YOU place the swords afterwards with the hand tool (the main hand = the far arm, sword behind the body; the off hand = the near arm, in front). So the fists must be where the swords should be: in frames 3 and 4 the NEAR fist is raised in front of the face (the sword will stand up from it, a little tilted, covering the face and chest) and the FAR fist is close beside it at chest height (a second sword can cross the first one). Nothing else is drawn in front of the fists.
