THE RULE FOR WHAT GOES INTO THE SPRITE
- IN THE SPRITE: everything CRISP and OPAQUE with a clean edge: the flame crown, flame tongues, embers as small
  solid flames, stylised smoke as solid cartoon clouds with an outline, crystals, spikes, hairline arcs, star
  sparkles, ink wisps.
- LEFT OUT of the sprite: everything SOFT, GLOWING or TRANSPARENT: glow, halo, bloom, light rays, heat haze,
  frost mist, soft see-through smoke. Why: the sheets are drawn on a flat magenta background and cut out by
  colour; a soft or glowing edge cannot be separated from the background (it stays as a pink halo). Those parts
  come later as separate painted layers (like the mage's skill textures, drawn additively over the sprite).
Because the effects are different in every panel (flames flicker, smoke drifts, arcs jump) the animation gets a
natural flicker; only the BODY has to stay the same from panel to panel.

THE PROMPT (paste everything between the lines)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OUTPUT: make exactly ONE still image. No GIF, no animation, no extra images.

Make a character reference picture of a small monster for a 2D fantasy RPG: the FIRE SLIME from the attached
picture, with its flames and smoke. Keep the same painted game-sprite style, thick dark outlines, colours,
blisters and face. Do not redesign the character.

CHARACTER, THE BODY: a low dome of glossy molten orange-red jelly, wider than tall (the width is about 1.7 times
the height of the body, like the attached picture). Its underside is a flat, wavy, skirt-like edge that spreads
out on the ground in soft scallops. Thick dark red-brown outline around the body, a lighter yellow-orange rim
light along the lower edge, and glossy yellow-orange blisters (bubbles) of different sizes scattered over the
body exactly like the attached picture. The face: heavy slanted angry brows, two narrow slanted eyes with glowing
yellow pupils, and a wavy grimace with two small bumps at the corners. No arms, no legs, no ears, no horns.

CHARACTER, ITS FIRE (drawn like the attached picture, but crisp): a crown of tall flames burning on top of its
head, and one flame tongue licking up on each side of the body. Every flame is painted with a bright yellow core,
an orange body and red tips, and has a clean dark red-brown outline like the body's, fully opaque. A few small
solid flames or embers float just above the crown. Behind and above the flames, two or three puffs of smoke, drawn
as SOLID, fully opaque, stylised cartoon clouds in dark grey with a darker grey outline and simple flat shading.
The flames and the smoke are part of the creature and stay close to it: the flames rise at most about one body
height above the dome, the smoke a little higher.

NO SOFT EFFECTS: no glow, no light halo, no bloom, no lens flare, no heat haze, no blur, no soft or see-through
smoke, no gradient fade-outs, no light spilling onto the background. Every part of the picture has a crisp,
hard, opaque edge. No fire on the floor, no puddle, no cast shadow.

VIEW: a three-quarter view, the whole body and the face turned about 30 degrees towards the RIGHT edge of the
picture, as if it were looking at an enemy standing on the right. The base rests flat on an invisible floor.

SIZE AND FLOOR: the slime is centred. The body (the dome) is about 45% of the picture width; the lowest point of
its base is at about 82% of the picture height; with the flames and smoke the whole creature is at most 70% of
the picture height. At least 10% empty margin on every side; nothing touches an edge.

BACKGROUND: one flat pure magenta (#FF00FF) background filling the whole picture, identical in every pixel. No
gradient, ground line, shadow, reflection, glow or vignette. No text, labels or borders. No magenta or pink
anywhere on the creature itself; the outlines are dark red-brown or dark grey and hard-edged.

FINAL REMINDER: exactly one image; the fire slime with crisp opaque flames and solid cartoon smoke; no glow, no
haze, no soft edges; facing right; flat magenta background.
