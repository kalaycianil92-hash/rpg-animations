WHY. The four elemental slimes (fire, ice, lightning, dark) are one creature in four elements. The Elite is different on purpose (the user, 2026-11-09): a slime of STONE in BODY ARMOUR, holding a SWORD and a SHIELD, with no real hands (one blob-like fist holds the sword; the hand that holds the shield is never seen), and its main feature is PHYSICAL attacks, so it carries no element, no aura and no glowing parts. THE IDEA: a grey stone slime that has learned to fight like a knight. Body: matte grey stone with hairline cracks, chips and mineral flecks (it still sags and squashes like a slime). Shield: a big heater shield of dark steel with a bronze rim and a stone boss, held in front of the body on the near (left) side, so its hand is hidden behind it. Sword: a worn steel sword held upright by a rounded blob fist on a short stalk of the slime's own stone material, on the enemy side (right), beside the face and never across it.

RULES FOR THE LATER ANIMATIONS (they decide the drawing now): the ARMOUR, the SHIELD and the SWORD are rigid, hard-edged objects (they keep their shape in every frame and only ride with the body); only the grey stone body squashes and stretches. The face stays bare (no helmet, no visor) so the hit, death and dodge frames can change it. Keep the sword and the shield clearly apart, with the face between them, so a swing never has to cross the shield.

BACKGROUND IS MAGENTA (#FF00FF) as for the fire, ice and lightning slimes (the creature is grey, steel and bronze; none of it is pink, magenta or purple, so the key is safe).
