WHY THIS ONE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER SLIMES' ATTACKS. The fire, ice, lightning and dark slimes spit from where they stand: their body squashes and stretches and the page adds the effect. The Elite is the first slime that really swings something, so the sheet needs a real POSE CHANGE: the stone fist lifts the sword up and back, the body rears and leans back, then the whole body drops and leans forward as the sword chops down in front of it, then it recovers. Everything that is hard stays rigid: the back-plate, the belt, the shield and the sword never bend or change size (they only ride with the body when it leans). The page adds what a sprite cannot hold: the Elite STEPS IN toward the target (the sheet does not move the creature along the floor: the base stays on the same spot in every panel), the slash trail, the dust at the impact and the shake. So NONE of that is drawn.

THE SWING (important, the cell the game uses is a fixed square): the sword is long (about two thirds of the dome's height). To keep the swing inside the picture, the chop ends with the blade pointing STEEPLY DOWN in front of the Elite (not stretched out horizontally): the sword is described below with the hands of a clock, 12 = straight up, 3 = pointing horizontally toward the enemy on the right, 6 = straight down. The sword goes 12:30 (rest) - 12 - 11 - 10:30 (wind-up, tip leaning back) - 2 (mid swing) - 5 (the strike) - 5:30 (follow-through) - 1 (recover).

THE FIST FLOATS (the user's decision, 2026-11-09): the stone fist with fingers that holds the sword has no arm, no stalk and no wrist, and it is not joined to the body in ANY panel: there is always a clear gap of flat background between the fist (and the sword) and the body, the shield, the back-plate and the round plate behind the face. The fist and the sword are ONE rigid piece, the same in every panel (same fist, same sword, same size, same blade length); they only change place and turn as one piece.

THE BACKGROUND IS MAGENTA (#FF00FF) as in the look picture (the creature is grey stone, steel, bronze and brown leather with amber eyes; none of it is pink, magenta or purple).
